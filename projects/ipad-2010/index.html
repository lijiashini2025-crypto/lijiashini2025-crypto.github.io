LIGHT THE SPARK WITHIN

From a touch, to a brighter you.

Reimagining the 2010 iPad launch as a journey people can enter, shape and carry forward.

这是一份概念发布会策划：从第一次触碰，到共同点亮世界。IGNITE THE JOURNEY ↓
01 / The challenge

Why rethink
the launch?

A new category needed a story people could feel before they saw the product.

为什么重新设计发布？我们希望观众先体验“触碰带来创造”，再认识 iPad。

01 / PERCEPTION

More than a larger iPhone

A device without a distinct story risks being understood only through an existing one.没有新的故事，iPad 容易只被看成“放大的 iPhone”。

02 / PRESENTATION

Beyond one voice

Different creators and contexts could make the possibilities tangible together.单人演示难以让不同观众看见自己的使用情境。

03 / CONTINUITY

Beyond the reveal

A launch moment could become a continuing invitation to participate.发布的高潮之后，还需要一个让人持续参与的理由。

02 / The big idea

LIGHT THE
SPARK WITHIN

iPad 不是创造力本身，而是唤醒一个人原本已经拥有的潜能。

Strategic logic

From product demo
to participatory journey.

A theme became an action.

People could physically make a spark.口号变成可触碰的动作。

Participation came first.

The audience entered the idea before the reveal.产品亮相之前，观众已参与其中。

Features formed a story.

Each use added another connection.每个功能都为整体故事增加一条光路。

Creation became collective.

Personal light expanded into a shared map.个人作品最终汇成集体地图。

这个方案的优势，在于同一个核心行为贯穿所有触点，而不是仅靠一句口号维持统一。
