Stadium Participation

Turn spectators into participants.

The Memory Wall and Try Tunnel let fans add a memory and physically cross into the next 50 years.让赛事不只被观看：观众通过回忆墙与体验隧道，成为周年故事的一部分。

City Exploration

Turn Hong Kong into the second stage.

The City Passport carries the festival from Kai Tak to local dining, nightlife and landmark journeys.把狂欢从启德延伸到餐饮、夜生活与城市地标，带动香港文旅体验。

Shared Value

Turn participation into lasting value.

Stamps, UGC and partner rewards connect fan movement with shared memories, tourism footfall and commercial value.用印章、UGC 与品牌奖励连接用户到访、内容传播及商业价值。

Reflection

What Makes
The Idea Work

A rugby term became a life metaphor.“Try” links a scoring move with the courage to attempt something new.

The anniversary became urgency, not nostalgia.The 50th year gives fans a once-only reason to join the 2026 moment.

The stadium expanded into the city.City touchpoints turn Hong Kong into a second stage beyond Kai Tak.

Sponsors became part of the experience, not just logos.Cathay and HSBC power journeys, stamps and rewards inside the participation loop.
